# Design attribution

This skill adapts architectural ideas rather than copying a single upstream workflow verbatim.

- TauricResearch/TradingAgents, Apache-2.0: specialized analysts, bull/bear debate, risk review, structured action output, verified market snapshots and decision reflection. https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents
- xbtlin/ai-berkshire, MIT: value-investing research stages, information-richness grading, multi-source financial checks, scenario valuation, checklists, thesis tracking and report discipline. https://github.com/xbtlin/ai-berkshire

The A-share/U.S.-stock execution policy, lot validation and combined research-to-order workflow in this skill are custom adaptations. Preserve upstream attribution if redistributing substantial copied portions in future versions.
